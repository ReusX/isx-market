About the RSISX Index — مؤشر ربيع للأوراق المالية

The Rabee Securities ISX Index (RSISX) is the primary benchmark for the Iraq Stock Exchange. Published by Rabee Securities, it tracks ISX-listed stocks weighted by market capitalization, denominated in Iraqi Dinars (IQD) and updated daily during trading sessions.

Related indices: RSISXTR (total return), RSIHX (hospitality), RSIBX (banking sector).

🏦
About the Iraq Stock Exchange (ISX)

The Iraq Stock Exchange (بورصة العراق / ISX) is Iraq's official securities market, headquartered in Baghdad and operating under the Iraq Securities Commission (ISC). Trading runs Sunday–Thursday, reflecting the Iraqi work week.

Established in its modern form in 2004, replacing the Baghdad Stock Exchange (BSE). It lists 104 companies across banking, telecom, industry, tourism, insurance, agriculture, and services.

📡
Telecommunications — قطاع الاتصالات

Asiacell (TASC) — One of Iraq's three major mobile operators, majority-owned by Ooredoo (Qatar). Among the largest companies by market cap on the ISX.

Al-Khatem Telecom / Zain Iraq (TZNI) — Iraqi subsidiary of Zain Group (Kuwait). Holds the largest paid-up capital on the ISX at over 1.8 trillion IQD.
